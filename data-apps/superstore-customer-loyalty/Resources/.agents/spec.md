# Superstore Customer Loyalty — spec

Replaces the "tabNext Embed" tab on the embedding_playbook Superstore demo.
Audience: demo viewers (retail account managers). Question: which customers are slipping away and
what are they worth?

- Headline: lifetime sales held by At Risk customers.
- KPI strip: customers, YoY retention, Champions, at-risk sales.
- Loyalty tiers (click to filter the customer list).
- Retention (prior year -> latest year) by Segment and primary Region, with an overall reference line.
- Customer list for the selected tier (top 25 by lifetime sales), click for drill-in:
  sales by year, category mix, recent orders.
